      About this prototype and your records

      Nine authored practice equations and nine paired questions cover warm-up, signed numbers, fractions and multi-step work. Equation equivalence is checked locally using exact arithmetic. Worked hints come from checked content; the language-model response boundary is simulated and no external AI is connected.

      Operation counts and outcomes can be saved on this computer. Your free-text working and session access tokens are excluded from the saved trace. The software checks and sample CSV files are developer demonstrations. A participant study remains future work.
